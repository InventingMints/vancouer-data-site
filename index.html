FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers.

Data protection means having our team assess how your organisation collects, stores, uses, and protects personal and business data. PIPEDA and similar privacy regulations apply to most businesses handling personal information, and non-compliance carries real regulatory and reputational risk. If you hold customer, employee, or partner data in any form, you need a framework.

AI readiness means your data is clean, well-governed, and structured in a way that AI tools can actually use effectively. Most AI implementations underperform not because the AI is weak, but because the underlying data is inconsistent, incomplete, or ungoverned. We assess your readiness before you commit budget to tools that may fail to deliver.

Data management and governance covers how your organisation defines, owns, stores, quality-checks, and controls access to its data. It includes creating data policies, assigning data ownership, establishing quality standards, managing data across its lifecycle, and putting the accountability structures in place that keep your data trustworthy as your business grows. Without it, AI tools fail, compliance risk rises, and decisions get made on unreliable information.
