AI Readiness & Strategy

How a professional services firm avoided $200K in wasted AI spend

IndustryProfessional Services
Size45 employees
Timeline6 weeks

The Challenge

A mid-size professional services firm was ready to deploy an AI-powered document processing and client intake system. They had budget approved and had already begun vendor conversations. But before committing, they wanted an independent assessment of whether their data infrastructure could actually support it.

What We Found

Their client data was spread across four disconnected systems with no unified structure, inconsistent naming conventions, and thousands of duplicate records. The AI tools they had selected required clean, structured input data. Without remediation, the implementation would have failed within three months — or produced dangerously inaccurate outputs.

What We Did

We delivered a full AI readiness assessment covering data quality scoring, architecture gaps, governance posture, and a phased remediation roadmap. We renegotiated their vendor timeline and helped them fix the foundation first — restructuring their core data layer over eight weeks before AI implementation began.

  • Prevented an estimated $200K implementation failure before it happened
  • Reduced duplicate records by 87% across all client systems
  • AI deployment launched on schedule, on budget, 4 months later
  • Document processing time reduced by 62% post-deployment

"We were weeks away from signing a contract that would have failed. The readiness assessment was the most valuable thing we did before our AI project."

Data Management & Governance

A manufacturer cut reporting time by 70% with a governance framework

IndustryManufacturing
Size120 employees
Timeline10 weeks

The Challenge

A growing manufacturer had data sitting in three separate ERP systems, two spreadsheet-based tracking tools, and a legacy inventory platform. Every monthly leadership report required two full days of manual consolidation by the operations manager — and the numbers still didn't always reconcile.

What We Did

We mapped every data source, defined data ownership for each domain, built a governance policy covering data quality standards, naming conventions, and update responsibilities. We then designed a unified data layer that pulled from all systems automatically, with a single approved version of truth for leadership reporting.

The Outcome

Monthly reporting dropped from two days to four hours. Leadership had confidence in the numbers for the first time in three years. Within six months, the governance framework expanded to cover their new production line data without any additional consulting engagement.

  • Monthly reporting time reduced from 2 days to under 4 hours
  • Single source of truth established across all 5 data systems
  • Data ownership assigned across 8 departments
  • Zero reconciliation errors in the first 6 months post-implementation

"I used to dread the end of every month. Now the report practically generates itself and I trust every number in it."

Data Protection & Compliance

A health-adjacent clinic went from zero documentation to audit-ready in 8 weeks

IndustryHealth & Wellness
Size22 employees
Timeline8 weeks

The Challenge

A wellness clinic handling personal health information had no formal privacy policy, no data classification system, and no documented process for how client information was stored or accessed. A new enterprise client required a PIPEDA compliance confirmation before signing a corporate wellness contract worth $180K annually.

What We Did

We completed a full data protection audit, classified all data assets, identified and closed three critical exposure points, wrote their privacy policy from scratch, implemented access controls across their booking and records systems, and produced a compliance documentation package audit-ready for review.

The Outcome

The enterprise client signed within two weeks of receiving the compliance documentation. The clinic now has a permanent privacy framework that scales with them and requires only a quarterly review — not a full rebuild every time a new client asks.

  • $180K annual enterprise contract secured directly from compliance work
  • 3 critical data exposure points identified and remediated
  • Full PIPEDA-aligned documentation package delivered in 8 weeks

A national retailer grew sales 20% while 40% of locations were closed

IndustryRetail
SizeMulti-location
Timeline12 weeks

The Challenge

When the pandemic forced closure of nearly half their brick-and-mortar locations, this national retailer faced an impossible problem: how to maintain revenue without visibility into which products were moving, where demand was shifting, or how to redeploy inventory across the network in real time.

What We Did

We designed and built a unified data intelligence layer that consolidated inventory, customer behaviour, and sales data from all locations into a single real-time dashboard. Leadership could see stock levels, demand signals, and fulfilment gaps in minutes — not days. This enabled dynamic stock reallocation across their network as demand shifted.

The Outcome

Instead of contracting during closures, they grew. Real-time intelligence let them outpace competitors who were flying blind. The intelligence layer became a permanent fixture of their operations and now supports their e-commerce expansion.

Bookkeeping & Financial Records

An e-commerce business raised $320K after 18 months of clean books

IndustryE-commerce
Size8 employees
TimelineOngoing

The Challenge

A fast-growing e-commerce business had been running on a mix of personal and business accounts, with receipts in email, expenses untracked, and no reconciled records for the prior 18 months. They wanted to raise investment capital but had nothing to show investors. Their accountant had warned them twice that tax season would be a crisis.

What We Did

We performed a full financial records reconstruction for the prior 18 months — categorising every transaction, reconciling every account, and separating personal from business expenses. We implemented a proper bookkeeping system with monthly close processes, expense policies, and automated reconciliation. From month one going forward, books were clean, current, and investor-presentation ready.

The Outcome

The founders walked into their first investor meeting with 18 months of clean, auditable financial records. They closed a $320K seed round four months later. Their accountant reported the smoothest tax filing in the company's history. Monthly bookkeeping is now a 30-minute review, not a weekend crisis.

  • $320K seed round raised — clean books were a key investor requirement
  • 18 months of financial records reconstructed and reconciled
  • Personal and business finances fully separated for the first time
  • Monthly close now takes 30 minutes, not a full weekend

"We couldn't have raised without clean books. What felt like a nightmare task got done properly and fast — and we haven't worried about our finances since."
